Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026 • Effective Immediately

At AgileLab, we take the confidentiality, privacy, and integrity of your team conversations, media streams, and sprint collaboration data with the highest level of engineering rigor. This Privacy Policy details our data minimization practices, encryption standards, and your rights.

1. Data Minimization & Information We Collect

We strictly collect only information necessary to operate real-time messaging, audio/video conferencing, and sprint assistance:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, password hash (salted using BCrypt), full display name, and avatar seed.
  • Collaboration Data: Chat messages, group associations, task action items, and meeting transcription text submitted by users.
  • Presence & Session Telemetry: Active connection state, last-seen timestamps, and ephemeral WebRTC SDP handshake signaling tokens.

2. WebRTC Peer-to-Peer Media Privacy

Direct 1-on-1 voice and video sessions leverage WebRTC peer-to-peer protocols. Media streams (audio and video) flow directly between authorized participant endpoints using DTLS/SRTP encryption. Our servers only facilitate the initial STOMP connection exchange (ICE candidates and session descriptions) and do not store raw audio or video stream bytes.

3. Spring AI & LLM Data Boundaries

AI features—including chat summarization, semantic question answering (RAG), and action item extraction—execute within your isolated workspace context. Your conversations are never used to train third-party public machine learning models.

4. Security & Data Retention

All transmission is protected via TLS/HTTPS and WSS protocols. Passwords are never stored in plaintext. We implement strict Content Security Policies, connection throttling, and session timeouts. You maintain the right to delete messages, purge action items, or request full account termination at any time.

5. Contact Information

For privacy queries, data requests, or compliance audits, contact our Data Protection Team at privacy@agilelab.io or call +1 (800) 555-0199.
